Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Introduction

Khair Allah Medical Tourism ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use our website and services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you contact us or use our services:

  • Full name, email address, phone number, and country
  • Details about your medical requirements or travel preferences
  • Any documents or medical records you choose to share with us
  • Technical data such as IP address and browser type (automatically collected)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and arrange medical tourism services
  • Coordinate with hospitals, doctors, and logistics partners on your behalf
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share relevant information only with:

  • Partner hospitals and medical professionals (with your consent)
  • Travel, accommodation, and transfer providers necessary for your journey
  • Service providers who help us operate the website (under strict confidentiality)
  • Authorities when required by law

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We keep your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

8. Cookies

Our website may use essential cookies to ensure proper functioning. We do not use cookies for advertising or extensive tracking at this time.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the effective date.
